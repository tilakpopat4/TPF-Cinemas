## Plain-English summary

This summary helps you understand the agreement. The numbered clauses below are what legally applies.

- **You keep ownership** of your film. We are only borrowing the right to show it.
- **The licence is non-exclusive.** You can still send your film to festivals, upload it to YouTube, or license it to other platforms.
- **You choose the timing.** If a festival needs your film to stay offline, tell us and we will hold or limit publication.
- **You can leave.** You can ask us to remove your film at any time, with [30] days' notice.
- **You get credit.** Your name appears as director on your film page and your filmmaker profile.
- **You promise the film is yours to license.** That includes the music, the cast and crew permissions, and the locations.
- **We review before publishing.** We may say no, but if we ask for changes we will tell you exactly what and why.

## 2. The licence you give us

2.1 You give us a **non-exclusive, non-transferable** (except as clause 15 allows) licence, for the Term and in the Territory, to:

- (a) stream and publicly display the Film online, in whole, through the Platform;
- (b) host, encode, store and deliver the Film using third-party providers (for example video hosting and delivery services, or YouTube), provided each provider is used only to run the Platform;
- (c) embed the Film in the Platform's website and apps;
- (d) create and share promotional excerpts, trailers, thumbnails and screenshots totalling no more than [90] seconds of moving footage, for promoting the Film and the Platform;
- (e) add subtitles, captions, age-rating labels and technical formatting needed to show the Film properly; and
- (f) use your name, bio, photograph and credits, as you provide them, to promote the Film and your profile on the Platform.

2.2 We **will not**: edit, cut, dub or change the content of the Film (apart from clause 2.1(d) and (e)); allow viewers to download the Film unless you separately agree; sell the Film; license it to any third party for their own use; or use the Film in advertising for products unrelated to the Platform.

2.3 The licence is **royalty-free** unless clause 9 says otherwise.

## 3. What you keep

3.1 You remain the owner of the Film and all rights in it. Nothing in this agreement transfers ownership to us.

3.2 Because the licence is **non-exclusive**, you may license, distribute, screen or sell the Film anywhere else, at any time, including festivals, other streaming services and your own channels, without asking us.

3.3 **Festival holds.** If a festival or other opportunity requires the Film to be unpublished, private or restricted, tell us. We will, on request and within [7] days, either delay publication or restrict access (for example by region) for the period you specify, up to [12] months. Checking each festival's rules is your responsibility.

## 4. Term and territory

4.1 **Territory:** worldwide, unless Schedule A states a different territory.

4.2 **Term:** the number of months stated in Schedule A (the default is [24] months), starting on the Publication Date.

4.3 The licence **does not renew automatically**. Before it ends, we may ask you to renew it, and it continues only if you agree in writing (email or in-app confirmation is enough).

4.4 If we approve the Film but do not publish it within [90] days of approval, either of us may cancel this agreement by notice.

## 5. Your promises (warranties)

You confirm that:

5.1 you are at least 18 years old, or have your parent's or guardian's written consent, and you have the authority to enter into this agreement;

5.2 you own, or have all permissions needed for, everything in the Film and the materials you provide, including script, footage, performances, artwork and music, and you can grant the licence in clause 2;

5.3 you have written permission (releases) from everyone who appears in the Film or contributed to it, including cast, crew, musicians and location or property owners, and their permissions allow online streaming worldwide for the Term;

5.4 **all music and sound recordings** in the Film are original, licensed for online streaming worldwide for the Term, royalty-free, or in the public domain, exactly as you declare in Schedule C, and you can show us proof on request;

5.5 the Film does not infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy or other rights, and does not defame anyone or break any law;

5.6 if anyone under 18 appears in the Film, their parent or guardian has consented in writing and the Film complies with the law on protecting children;

5.7 the age rating you choose in Schedule A honestly reflects the Film's content; and

5.8 everything you tell us in Schedule A and Schedule C is true and complete.

## 6. Content standards and age rating

6.1 The Film must follow the Content Standards in Schedule B.

6.2 You classify the Film using the rating categories shown on the Platform (**U, UA 7+, UA 13+, UA 16+, A**). We may change the rating, add content descriptors, or place the Film behind an age check, if we reasonably think the rating is wrong. We will tell you when we do.

6.3 We must follow the rules that apply to online content platforms in India, and may need to remove or restrict content to do so.

## 7. Review and publication

7.1 Submitting the Film does not guarantee publication. Our curators decide, at our discretion, whether to publish.

7.2 We will review each submission and tell you the outcome. If we ask for changes or decline the Film, we will give you written feedback explaining why.

7.3 Before publishing, we may check your music declaration and other documents. We will not publish the Film until we are satisfied that the licence and rights information is in order.

7.4 We will normally decide within [21] days of receiving a complete submission, but this is a target, not a guarantee.

## 8. Credit and promotion

8.1 We will credit you as director on the Film's page, in the Platform's listings, and on your filmmaker profile, and we will not remove or change the credits you provide.

8.2 If you tell us the Film is your first film, and our curators agree, we may label it "Debut" or similar.

8.3 We will make reasonable efforts to promote the Film, but we do not promise any particular audience, views or results.

## 10. Removing the Film

10.1 **You can withdraw the Film** at any time by giving us [30] days' notice (email or in-app request). We will remove it from the Platform within that period.

10.2 After removal, we may keep copies for legal and back-up purposes only, and will not show them to the public. We will take reasonable steps to remove the Film from third-party hosts we control. Promotional excerpts already shared on social media may remain visible for a reasonable time while we remove them, and we are not responsible for copies made by others.

10.3 **We can remove the Film immediately** if: we receive a valid legal complaint, court order or government direction; someone credibly claims that the Film infringes their rights; we reasonably believe you have broken clause 5 or 6; or leaving it up would create legal risk for us. Where the law allows, we will tell you why.

10.4 Complaints about content on the Platform can be sent to [grievance officer name and email], and we will respond within the time required by law.

## 11. Viewers, feedback and data

11.1 We may let viewers rate and comment on the Film. Comments belong to the people who wrote them, and we may hide or remove any comment that breaks our community rules.

11.2 We will share viewing statistics and feedback for your Film with you. You may use viewer information only to understand your audience, and not to contact viewers directly unless they have agreed.

11.3 We handle personal data under our Privacy Policy and the data protection laws that apply in India.

## 12. Third-party services

12.1 We may use third-party services to host or deliver the Film. Their own terms also apply to us, and we are not responsible for their outages or policy changes.

12.2 If the Film is shown through YouTube, YouTube's rules and automated systems (including copyright claims) may apply to it. You will tell us promptly if you receive any claim about the Film.

## 13. Responsibility

13.1 If a third party makes a claim against us because you broke a promise in clause 5, you will compensate us for the reasonable losses, legal costs and damages that result, and we will tell you promptly about the claim and give you a fair chance to help resolve it.

13.2 We provide the Platform "as is". We do not promise that it will always be available, error-free, or protected against piracy, although we will take reasonable steps to protect the Film.

13.3 Our total liability to you under or in connection with this agreement is limited to [the amount we have paid you in the last 12 months / ₹[amount]], except where the law does not allow a limit (for example fraud or wilful misconduct).

## 14. Ending this agreement

14.1 This agreement ends when the Term ends, when you withdraw the Film under clause 10.1, or when either of us cancels it under clause 14.2.

14.2 Either of us may end this agreement by notice if the other seriously breaks it and does not fix the problem within [15] days of being told.

14.3 When it ends, we stop showing the Film as described in clause 10. Clauses 3, 5, 10.2, 13, 16 and 17 continue to apply.

## 15. Changes of ownership

15.1 If you sell or transfer the Film's rights, this licence continues for the rest of the Term and binds the new owner. You will tell us and let the new owner know.

15.2 We may transfer this agreement only to a successor that takes over the Platform, and we will tell you. You may then withdraw the Film under clause 10.1.

## 16. Law and disputes

16.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

16.2 We will try to settle any dispute by talking to each other first, then by mediation if either of us asks. If that does not resolve it within [30] days, the dispute will be finally settled by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration is [Ahmedabad], and the language is English. The courts at [Ahmedabad] have jurisdiction over matters the law leaves to the courts.

## 17. General

17.1 **Whole agreement.** This agreement and its Schedules are the whole deal between us about the Film and replace anything said earlier.

17.2 **Changes.** We may release a new version of these terms for future submissions. A new version does not change a licence you have already signed unless you accept it.

17.3 **Notices.** Notices can be sent by email to the addresses in Schedule A, and count as received on the next working day.

17.4 **Electronic acceptance.** You can accept this agreement by ticking the acceptance box and submitting the form on the Platform. That counts as your electronic signature, and we will keep a record of the date, time and terms version you accepted.

17.5 **Independent parties.** This agreement does not make us partners, employer and employee, or agent and principal.

17.6 **If a clause fails.** If any part of this agreement is found unenforceable, the rest continues to apply.

17.7 **No waiver.** If we do not enforce a right immediately, we do not lose it.

The Film must not contain or promote:

1. sexual content involving anyone under 18, or any exploitation or abuse of children;
2. real, non-consensual sexual content, or sexually explicit material;
3. incitement to violence or terrorism, or content that promotes hatred against people because of religion, caste, race, gender, disability, region or sexual orientation;
4. real footage of serious violence or its aftermath, shown for shock value;
5. content that endangers national security, public order or the country's integrity, or that breaks Indian law;
6. private information about real people, or defamation of real people, shown without their consent;
7. instructions for weapons, self-harm or dangerous acts; or
8. anything you do not have the right to use.

Depictions of difficult subjects in a clearly artistic or documentary context may be accepted with an appropriate age rating and content descriptors. Our curators decide.

## Drafting notes for your lawyer

1. **Entity and address.** Fill in the legal entity that will operate TPF Cinemas. If Tilak Popat Films is a proprietorship, contracts are in your own name, so consider forming a company or LLP as the platform grows.
2. **State the territory and term explicitly.** Indian copyright law fills in defaults (a limited term and India-only territory) when a licence or assignment is silent. This draft states both, and your lawyer can confirm how the default rules apply.
3. **Stamp duty and e-signature.** Ask your lawyer whether the agreement needs stamping in Gujarat and whether tick-box acceptance is enough or an e-sign or e-stamp step is safer.
4. **IT Rules for online content publishers.** Confirm your obligations: self-classification, a grievance officer, the grievance timelines, and whether you fall under the rules as a "publisher of online curated content". The clause 6 rating labels and the clause 10.4 grievance contact are placeholders for this.
5. **YouTube use.** While films are shown through YouTube embeds, check that clause 12 and your setup fit YouTube's current terms, especially if you later add ads or subscriptions.
6. **Data protection.** Your Privacy Policy must match clause 11 and current Indian data protection law.
7. **Minors.** If filmmakers may be under 18, decide whether to require a guardian to accept this agreement.
8. **Liability and indemnity.** Ask whether clause 13's limits are reasonable and enforceable for your risk level.
9. **Payments.** Pick Option A or Option B in clause 9 before launch, and add tax-deduction language if Option B applies.
