<objective>
Build the creator legal agreement onboarding flow in `apps/studio`: database migration for creator-level master agreements, dynamic legal deed terms (Short vs. Feature Film), interactive vector canvas signature pad, client-side PDF compilation with embedded signature via `jspdf`, upload to Supabase private storage, and the onboarding intercept in the Studio dashboard.

Purpose: Protect TPF Cinemas legally by securing formal non-exclusive streaming grants and chain-of-title IP declarations from creators before content ingestion.
Output: Production-ready migration, `SignaturePad.tsx`, `LegalAgreementDoc.tsx`, `LegalOnboardingModal.tsx`, `pdfGenerator.ts`, and updated `App.tsx`.
</objective>
