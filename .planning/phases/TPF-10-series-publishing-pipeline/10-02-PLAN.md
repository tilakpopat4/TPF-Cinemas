<title>Define Series TypeScript Types and Data Fetching Hook</title>
<instruction>
1. Update `apps/studio/src/types/index.ts`:
   - Add `Series`, `Season`, `Episode`, `SeriesGenre`, `SeriesCredit`, `SeriesReview` interfaces.
2. Create `apps/studio/src/hooks/useSeries.ts`:
   - Fetch creator's series with season and episode counts, genres, and latest review.
   - Support `saveDraftSeries`, `saveSeasonsAndEpisodes`, `submitSeries`, and delete draft actions.

Run `npm --prefix apps/studio run build` to ensure types compile without issues.
</verify>

<task type="auto" id="2">
<title>Build Multi-Step Series Submission Wizard & Episode Builder</title>
<instruction>
1. Create `apps/studio/src/components/series/EpisodeBuilder.tsx`:
   - Interactive season tabs/manager ("Season 1", "+ Add Season").
   - Episode card list with reordering/numbering: Episode title, runtime in minutes, YouTube video link input (with auto-extraction of video ID and live preview modal), synopsis snippet, and thumbnail.
2. Create `apps/studio/src/components/series/NewSeriesModal.tsx`:
   - Multi-step wizard:
     - Step 1: Show Info (Title, Slug, Language, Age Rating, Synopsis, Creator Note, Poster [2:3], Backdrop [16:9]).
     - Step 2: Seasons & Episodes (renders `EpisodeBuilder`).
     - Step 3: Credits (Showrunner, Director, Writer, Key Cast).
     - Step 4: Rights & Declarations (Non-exclusive episodic streaming grant & clearance declaration).
     - Step 5: Final Review & Submit (Pre-submission sanity check and call to `submit_series` RPC).
</instruction>
<verify>
Verify wizard navigates between steps with validation at each stage, handling save draft and submit cleanly.
</verify>
</task>

<task type="auto" id="3">

</task>
