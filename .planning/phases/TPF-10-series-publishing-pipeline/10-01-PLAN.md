<title>Create Database Migration for Series, Seasons, and Episodes</title>
<instruction>
Write migration `supabase/migrations/20261010000001_series_publishing_pipeline.sql` with:
1. Tables:
   - `public.series`: `id`, `filmmaker_id`, `title`, `slug`, `synopsis`, `creator_note`, `language`, `age_rating`, `poster_url`, `backdrop_url`, `status`, `is_featured`, `published_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.
   - `public.seasons`: `id`, `series_id`, `season_number`, `title`, `synopsis`, `release_year`, `poster_url`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `unique(series_id, season_number)`.
   - `public.episodes`: `id`, `season_id`, `series_id`, `episode_number`, `title`, `synopsis`, `runtime_minutes`, `video_provider`, `video_ref`, `thumbnail_url`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `unique(season_id, episode_number)`.
   - `public.series_genres`: `series_id`, `genre_id`, `primary key (series_id, genre_id)`.
   - `public.series_credits`: `id`, `series_id`, `person_name`, `credit_role`, `sort_order`.
   - `public.series_reviews`: `id`, `series_id`, `reviewer_id`, `decision`, `notes`, `created_at`.
   - `public.episode_watch_history`: `user_id`, `episode_id`, `series_id`, `progress_seconds`, `completed`, `updated_at`, `primary key (user_id, episode_id)`.
   - `public.series_watchlist`: `user_id`, `series_id`, `added_at`, `primary key (user_id, series_id)`.
2. Triggers for `set_updated_at()` on series, seasons, and episodes.
3. RLS policies on all series tables:
   - Creator can select, insert, and update their own series/seasons/episodes when status in ('draft', 'changes_requested').
   - Staff can select all series/seasons/episodes and update review statuses.
   - Public can select series, seasons, and episodes where `series.status = 'published'`.
4. RPCs:
   - `submit_series(p_series_id uuid)`: Verifies caller owns series, checks at least 1 season with >= 1 episode with video_ref, transitions to 'submitted'.
   - `review_series(p_series_id uuid, p_decision public.review_decision, p_notes text)`: Curator review engine with mandatory feedback.
   - `publish_series(p_series_id uuid)`: Curator one-click publish transition to 'published' with timestamp.
5. Grants to `authenticated` and `anon` roles.
6. Schema reload: `NOTIFY pgrst, 'reload schema';`.
</instruction>

Ensure SQL parses cleanly, indexes are created on foreign keys and statuses, RLS policies are applied, and permissions granted.
