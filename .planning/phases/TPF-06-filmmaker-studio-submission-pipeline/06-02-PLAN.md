<threat_model>
- Threat: Viewing other filmmakers' review feedback or unreleased submissions.
  Mitigation: Supabase RLS enforces `film_reviews` can only be viewed by the film's owner or staff (`filmmaker_id = auth.uid() or is_staff()`).
- Threat: State desynchronization when curator updates film status during filmmaker session.
  Mitigation: Supabase Realtime channel subscription automatically triggers fresh fetch on any Postgres change.
</threat_model>
